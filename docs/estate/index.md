# Coordinate your estate

Two working items already raise shared questions: which runs first, where does the same source deploy, and what happened after the last change? Weaver keeps those relationships and results together. [Projects and estates](projects-and-estates.md) describes how the source, the installed catalogue and the physical Fabric items relate.

[Connect items](connected-items/index.md), [map development and production workspaces](environments/index.md), then [read Catalogue and Health](catalogue-and-health/index.md). The [Catalogue Dashboard](catalogue-dashboard.md) shows the same catalogue in Power BI. When another team's catalogue feeds yours, [work across teams' catalogues](across-catalogues.md) to inspect their dependencies and coordinate selected work.

Use [the development cycle](development-cycle.md) for ordinary edits and [run, test and inspect](operate/index.md) for the installed estate. [Recover failed work](recovery/index.md) by choosing the smallest action its state needs; [Mirror](mirror/index.md) is an optional development baseline, not the first step.

When you need finer control, [select part of the estate](selective-runs/index.md), [limit concurrency](concurrency.md) and [investigate performance](performance.md). [Workflows and controlled deployment](automation-and-deployment/index.md) takes that working loop into automation.
